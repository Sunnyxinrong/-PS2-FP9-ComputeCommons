## Research development beyond PS2

Next experiments should use measured action-buffer depletion, target motion, inference runtimes, network tails and fallback outcomes. Compare EDF, value-density scheduling, measured-system policies and an exact oracle under identical workloads. Test how solver overhead changes any gain, vary load and motion regimes, and include repeated trials and uncertainty. A paper targeting an international robotics/systems venue needs a substantive advance beyond substituting a physical deadline formula into an established auction.
